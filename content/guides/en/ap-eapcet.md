**AP EAPCET** (Engineering, Agriculture and Pharmacy Common Entrance Test) is the entrance test for first-year **B.Tech** (and Bio-Technology, Dairy Technology, Agricultural Engineering, Food Science and Technology), **B.Sc. (Agriculture / Horticulture)**, **B.V.Sc. & A.H**, **B.F.Sc**, **B.Pharmacy** and **Pharm.D** in Andhra Pradesh. JNTU Kakinada conducts it for the **Andhra Pradesh State Council of Higher Education (APSCHE)**. APSCHE's portal also runs the state's other entrance tests (ECET, ICET, PGECET, EdCET, LAWCET, PECET, PGCET and RCET), each with its own page. This guide follows the official **AP EAPCET 2026 Instruction Booklet** (Engineering) and the 2026 schedule; check the booklet for your year on the portal.

## Can I apply? {#eligibility}

From the 2026 instruction booklet:

- You are an **Indian national**, or a Person of Indian Origin (PIO) or Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) card holder.
- You **belong to Andhra Pradesh** and meet the **local / non-local status** rules in the Government of Andhra Pradesh orders for the year.
- You have at least **45% marks** (**40%** for reserved categories) in the required subjects taken together in the qualifying examination.
- **For Engineering, B.Pharmacy (MPC) and Pharm.D:** you passed **Intermediate (10+2)** with **Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry**, or a recognised equivalent, or a **Diploma in Engineering** from the State Board of Technical Education and Training, AP. If you are waiting for your final-year results you can still apply, but your rank counts only if you pass.
- **Age:** for B.Tech (Dairy Technology), B.Tech (Agricultural Engineering), B.Tech (Food Science and Technology) and B.Sc. (Agricultural Engineering), at least **17 years** by 31 December of the admission year, and at most **22** (**25** for SC and ST). For Pharm.D, at least 17 by 31 December.

Passing EAPCET does not by itself give you a seat: you must also meet the admission rules at counselling.

## Documents you will need {#documents}

**To fill in the online application**, keep ready:

- Hall ticket number of your qualifying examination (Intermediate or Diploma)
- Hall ticket number of your SSC (Class 10) or equivalent
- Date of birth
- Caste certificate (SC, ST and BC candidates)
- Aadhaar number
- Details of any special category claim (PH, NCC, sports and so on)
- Income certificate (up to ₹1 lakh, up to ₹2 lakh, or more than ₹2 lakh)
- Ration card
- Study, residence or other certificate proving your **local status** for the last 12 years

**On the exam day:**

- The **hall ticket**
- The **printed online application form**, with a recent colour photograph attested by a Gazetted Officer or the principal of the college where you studied
- One **photo ID**: Intermediate hall ticket, passport, PAN card, voter ID, Aadhaar card or driving licence
- A ball-point pen
- An attested copy of your caste certificate (SC and ST candidates only)

**At counselling**, Proformas I to III in the booklet are used to claim nativity, community and local status.

## The fee {#fee}

The registration fee in 2026 was **₹800** (open category), **₹750** (BC) and **₹700** (SC and ST), paid online by credit card, debit card, net banking or AP Online. Late fees in 2026 were ₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹4,000 and ₹10,000, each with a later deadline.

## Steps {#steps}

1. Open **cets.apsche.ap.gov.in** and choose **AP EAPCET** for the current year.
2. **Pay the registration fee** online. This is the first step.
3. **Fill in the online application form.** Choose the stream: **E** (Engineering), **AP** (Agriculture & Pharmacy), or **both**. If you want to write both, choose **Both** on one application, so you get the same test centre for the two tests. Submit **only one application**: more than one may lead to all being rejected.
4. **Check every detail and press Submit.** You get a filled-in form with your **Registration Number**. Use this number in all communication.
5. **Print the form.** You must hand it to the invigilator on the exam day, with your attested photograph pasted on it.
6. If you made a mistake, use the **correction window** (11 to 13 April in 2026).
7. **Download your hall ticket** from the portal or through **Mana Mitra**, the Andhra Pradesh government's WhatsApp service. The hall ticket tells you your exam date, session and centre: you cannot choose them.
8. **Write the test** (see below), then check the **preliminary key** and raise objections if needed (₹300 per question in 2026, refunded only for valid objections).
9. **Download your rank card** from the portal when the results are out.

## The test {#cbt}

- It is a **computer-based test** of **3 hours**: 160 multiple-choice questions, **80 Mathematics, 40 Physics, 40 Chemistry** (Engineering stream). Each question carries one mark, and there is **no negative marking**.
- Questions are in **English and Telugu**. If a Telugu question is unclear, the English one is final. Candidates who studied in Urdu medium and want Urdu translation help get a centre at Kurnool.
- A **mock test** is on the portal to practise the computer-based exam.
- **Reach the centre 2 hours early.** The registration desk closes 5 minutes before the start, and nobody is let in after the test begins, not even one minute late. You cannot leave before the end.
- **Not allowed:** calculators, mobile phones, electronic watches, log tables, papers or any other material. Carrying them is treated as unfair means.

## Rank and admission {#after}

- To qualify you need **25% of the maximum marks** used for ranking. SC and ST candidates have no minimum, but their admission is limited to the reserved seats.
- Your rank is based on **EAPCET normalised marks (75%)** and **Intermediate marks (25%)**.
- The rank is valid for admissions in that academic year only. **Keep your filled-in application, hall ticket and rank card** for verification.
- Seats are allotted through **counselling** run by the **Commissioner of Technical Education**, Andhra Pradesh. The counselling information booklet with colleges and seats is published later on the portal.
