**Jnanabhumi** is the Andhra Pradesh portal for **post-matric scholarships**: the **reimbursement of tuition fee (RTF)** and the **maintenance fee (MTF)** for students in colleges after Class 10. The scheme has run online since 2008-09 and on Jnanabhumi since 2017-18. **You do not apply on the portal yourself**: your **college** files your application in its login, and your **village or ward secretariat** verifies it. This guide follows the Government's orders on the scheme, **G.O.Ms.No.18, Social Welfare (Edn) Dept., 04-04-2022** (duties and timelines of everyone involved) and **G.O.Ms.No.87, Social Welfare (Edu.I) Dept., 20-11-2024** (how the fee is paid), and the notices on the portal.

## Can I apply? {#eligibility}

- The scheme is for students in **post-matric courses** (after Class 10) in colleges registered on Jnanabhumi. Your college must be **registered or renewed** on the portal for the year.
- Eligibility is checked by the portal's **six-step validation**, against income-related criteria. The portal's checks include four-wheeler ownership, government employment, income tax payment, land and urban property.
- If you were admitted through an entrance test (CET), your admission is checked against the CET convener's data. The CET allotment order says fee exemption is only for those found eligible, and a student who has already completed a course of the **same or a higher level** is **not eligible**.
- Students studying **in Telangana or other states** apply online on Jnanabhumi and send the hard copies by registered post to the District Welfare Officer of their native district, as per the guidelines on the portal.

## Documents you will need {#documents}

- The **documents your college asks for** (the "prescribed documents"), handed to the principal **within 7 days of admission**
- Your **Aadhaar**, for the one-time **biometric authentication**
- Your **CET allotment order**, if you were admitted through an entrance test and your name is not found in the CET data
- **ST students:** your **One Time Registration (OTR)** details: the portal says all ST students must complete OTR before submitting the scholarship application

## Steps {#steps}

1. **Hand your documents to the principal** within **7 days of admission**. If you do not, you lose the claim for the year.
2. **ST students only:** first complete the **One Time Registration (OTR)** from the link on Jnanabhumi; the portal has an OTR user manual.
3. Your **college files your application** in its Jnanabhumi login (fresh or renewal) within 15 days of admission. When it is filed, **select the village or ward secretariat** where your family regularly lives and is mapped; a wrong secretariat delays verification.
4. **Check every detail** of the application, especially your course, before you give your **one-time biometric authentication**. First-time applicants do it at a **village or ward secretariat or a MeeSeva centre**; others at the college. Do it within **7 days** of the college uploading the application.
5. **Be available for verification** by the **Welfare and Education Assistant** (village secretariat) or the **Ward Education and Data Processing Secretary** (ward secretariat). Eligibility is verified **every year** until the course ends.
6. **Attend the social audit** at your secretariat and check the **eligible / ineligible lists** displayed there. If you are wrongly listed as ineligible, **file an objection with evidence** within the time given. If you miss it, you lose the claim for the course.
7. If you get an **SMS** asking for your CET allotment order, **upload it within 7 days** in your login or through the college.

## After you apply {#after}

- **Attendance:** you need at least **75% attendance**, and daily biometric attendance in the college where it is notified, or the scholarship is not released.
- **How the tuition fee is paid** (G.O.Ms.No.87, 2024): from 2024-25, the tuition fee of all students **other than SC students** is paid **to the college's account**, and from 2025-26 this depends on the college's biometric (FRS) attendance. For **SC students**, the tuition fee is still paid **to the student's account**, as per Government of India guidelines.
- If the money is credited to your family's account, the 2022 orders say the fee must be **paid to the college within 7 days**, and the receipt acknowledged with biometric e-acknowledgement within 14 days; otherwise later instalments can be stopped.
- If you **change courses** in the middle of a course of the same level, you must pay back the scholarship received for the earlier course within 15 days, or the new application is not eligible.

## Questions and help {#help}

- Ask your **college principal** first: the college files and processes your application.
- For verification or objections, go to your **village or ward secretariat**. Objections are decided by the **MPDO** or **Municipal Commissioner**.
- The **District Welfare Officer** of your district verifies and approves applications.

## Good to know

- Colleges may not insist on fee payment in advance from a student eligible for the scheme (G.O.Ms.No.18, 2022).
- The 2022 order calls the scheme Jagananna Vidya Deevena (tuition fee) and Jagananna Vasathi Deevena (maintenance); the portal now calls it the post-matric scholarship. Names change; the process on Jnanabhumi is the same.
