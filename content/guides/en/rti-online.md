The **Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005** lets any Indian citizen ask a government office for information. For **Central Government** ministries, departments and other central public authorities, you can file your RTI online at **RTI Online**, run by the Department of Personnel & Training (DoPT).

## Before you start {#before}

- **This portal is only for the Central Government.** The portal warns that RTIs filed here for **State Government** offices (including the Government of NCT of Delhi) are **returned without refunding the fee**. For a state office, use that state's own RTI system.
- You do **not** need to create an account.

## Documents you will need {#documents}

- **An email address.** The portal sends you an OTP on it.
- **A mobile number** (optional, for SMS updates).
- **A BPL certificate**, only if you are below the poverty line and want to file free (PDF, up to 1 MB).
- **Your questions**, written clearly. The text box takes up to **3,000 characters**. Only letters, numbers and the characters `, . - _ ( ) / @ : & ? \ %` are allowed. A longer application can be uploaded as a PDF.
- **Do not upload** your Aadhaar card, PAN card or any other ID (the BPL card is the only exception).

## The fee {#fee}

- **₹10**, as prescribed in the RTI Rules, 2012. Pay by internet banking, a credit or debit card (Mastercard, Visa or RuPay) or UPI.
- **Free for citizens below the poverty line (BPL).** Choose **Yes** for "Is the Applicant Below Poverty Line?" and upload a copy of your BPL certificate (PDF, up to 1 MB).
- If the information costs more to provide (for example, many photocopies), the officer tells you the **additional fee** through the portal.

## Steps {#steps}

1. Open [RTI Online](https://rtionline.gov.in/) and click **Submit Request**.

   ![The RTI Online menu with "Submit Request" outlined in red](/static/guides/rti-online/1-home.webp "Screenshot of rtionline.gov.in, 14 Oct 2026. The red outline is ours.")

2. Read the guidelines, tick **I have read and understood the above guidelines**, and click **Submit**.

   ![The end of the RTI Online guidelines, with the "I have read and understood" box ticked and the Submit button outlined](/static/guides/rti-online/2-guidelines.webp "Screenshot of rtionline.gov.in, 14 Oct 2026. The red outline is ours.")

3. Enter your **email address**, your mobile number if you want SMS alerts, and the security code. Click **Submit**, then enter the **OTP** sent to your email.

   ![The Online RTI Request Form asking for an email address for the OTP, a mobile number and a security code](/static/guides/rti-online/3-form.webp "Screenshot of rtionline.gov.in, 14 Oct 2026.")

4. In the request form, choose the **Ministry/Department/Apex body** you want information from.
5. Fill in your details. Answer **"Is the Applicant Below Poverty Line?"** (Yes or No).
6. Type your questions in **Text for RTI Request application**, or upload them as a PDF under **Supporting document**.
7. Click **Make Payment** and pay ₹10. (BPL applicants click **Submit** instead.)
8. You get a **registration number** by email (and SMS if you gave your mobile). Keep it safe.

## After you file {#after}

- Your application goes to the **Nodal Officer** of that ministry, who sends it to the right **CPIO** (Central Public Information Officer).
- If you chose the wrong office, it is **transferred** to the right central office. You get a new registration number.
- If several officers hold the information, you may get **several registration numbers** for one application.
- **Check progress:** click **View Status**, enter your registration number and email, and confirm the OTP.
- The portal keeps your requests and appeals for **3 years**.

## If you get no reply or a bad reply: First Appeal {#appeal}

1. Click **Submit First Appeal** on the portal.
2. Enter the **registration number** and **email address** of your original application.
3. Choose your reason under **Ground For Appeal** and explain it (up to 3,000 characters, or upload a PDF).
4. Submit. **A first appeal is free.**

If the officer has not replied, you can appeal only after the **30-day** period is over. A first appeal can be filed online only for an RTI you filed online.

## If something goes wrong

- **Money deducted but no registration number:** do **not** pay again. Wait **24 to 48 working hours**, or check with **Payment Reconciliation** on the portal. If it still does not come, email **helprtionline-dopt[at]nic[dot]in** with your transaction details.
- **"Supporting documents required from applicant":** open **View Status**, and upload the document there.
- The helpline email is only for problems with the portal itself.
