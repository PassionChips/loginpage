This is a general overview. UIDAI decides which documents are accepted and what any fee is, so confirm both on the official portal.

## Before you start

- You usually need the mobile number linked to your Aadhaar, because a one-time password (OTP) is sent to it.
- Keep a supporting document for your new address ready. The portal lists which documents it accepts.

## Steps

1. Open the official **myAadhaar** portal and log in with your Aadhaar number and the OTP.
2. Choose the option to **update your address**.
3. Enter the new address and **upload the supporting document** the portal asks for.
4. Pay the fee if the portal shows one, then submit.
5. **Note the request number** shown after submitting. You can use it to check the status on the portal.

## If you cannot do it online

If your mobile number is not linked to your Aadhaar, the online process may not be available to you. In that case the official site explains how to visit an Aadhaar enrolment or update centre.

## Good to know

- Do not share your OTP with anyone, including people who offer to "help" with the update.
- Use only the official portal. Look-alike sites exist.
