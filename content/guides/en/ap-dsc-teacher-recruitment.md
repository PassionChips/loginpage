**DSC** (District Selection Committee) recruitment fills teacher posts in Andhra Pradesh's government schools. For 2026 it is run as the **Andhra Pradesh Teacher Recruitment Test (APTRT-2026)** by the **Director of School Education**, on **tet2dsc.apcfss.in**, the site that also runs the Teacher Eligibility Test (APTET). The **brief notifications** were issued on **13-10-2026**; the **detailed notification**, with qualifications, age limits, fees and dates, will be on the site **on or before 03-11-2026**. This guide follows the brief notifications, **G.O.Ms.Nos. 28, 29 and 30, School Education (Services-I) Dept., 13-10-2026**, and the official press note.

## What is being recruited {#posts}

The press note lists **2,721 tentative posts**:

| Recruitment | Posts | Vacancies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Government, Zilla Parishad, Mandal Parishad, Municipal schools | School Assistant (SA) 906, Secondary Grade Teacher (SGT) 1,205 | 2,111 |
| Special Education, in the same schools | SA (Special Education) 150, SGT (Special Education) 200 | 350 |
| Residential societies and other managements | Principal 30, PGT 81, TGT 113, Physical Director 2, PET 34 | 260 |

The societies are APSWREIS, APTWREIS, MJPAPBCWREIS, APREIS, AP Model Schools, and the Welfare of Differently Abled, Transgender & Senior Citizens Department. The press note's annexures give the **district-wise** and **society-wise** numbers. The numbers are **tentative**: the final post-wise, management-wise and cadre-wise vacancies with rosters will be those in the detailed notification, and later changes come as a corrigendum before the last date to apply.

## Can I apply? {#eligibility}

The **educational and professional qualifications, age limits and other eligibility conditions** will be in the **detailed notification** (on or before 03-11-2026). The recruitment follows the rules in force on the date of the detailed notification. Read it fully before applying.

## Documents you will need {#documents}

The certificates to upload will be listed in the **detailed notification**, due on or before 03-11-2026; this guide will list them once it is out. The official documents to read now, all on tet2dsc.apcfss.in:

- The **brief notification** for your posts: **General** (SA and SGT), **Societies** (Principal, PGT, TGT, PD, PET) or **Special Education**
- The **press note**, with the district-wise and society-wise vacancies
- **G.O.Ms.Nos. 28, 29 and 30** (13-10-2026), which permit the recruitment
- The **DSC-2026 syllabus** and the **Special Education syllabus**

## Steps {#steps}

1. **Read the brief notification and press note** for your post on **tet2dsc.apcfss.in** (DSC-2026 section).
2. **Study the syllabus**: the DSC-2026 syllabus and the Special Education syllabus are already on the site.
3. **Wait for the detailed notification**, due on or before **03-11-2026**, and read it carefully: it gives the exact vacancies, eligibility, fees and the application dates.
4. **Apply online only** on **tet2dsc.apcfss.in** during the period given in the detailed notification. Applications sent any other way are not accepted. Fill in every detail carefully.
5. **Apply well before the last date**, as the notification advises, to avoid the last-minute rush.
6. **Write the computer-based test** (objective type) on the date given in the detailed notification.

## After you apply {#after}

- Every new document on the DSC site (notifications, corrigenda, schedules, hall tickets, keys, results) appears on **AP DSC: what's new** on this site, which checks the site twice a day.
- The Government and the School Education Department can modify the recruitment details, including the number of vacancies; any change is notified as a corrigendum.

## Good to know

- The same site runs **APTET**, the Teacher Eligibility Test, and in 2026 a **Special TET for in-service teachers**; their notifications, schedules and FAQs are also listed on AP DSC: what's new.
- Use only tet2dsc.apcfss.in for the notification and the application.
