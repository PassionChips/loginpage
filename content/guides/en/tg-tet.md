The **Telangana Teacher Eligibility Test (TG TET)** is the test a teacher must pass to teach Classes I to VIII, as required under the Right to Education Act and the NCTE rules. The Department of School Education conducts **two regular TETs a year**, and in 2026 also **two Special TETs only for in-service teachers**. The current one is the **Special TGTET-In-service Teachers-2026-II**. This guide follows its **notification (Rc.No.444, 01-10-2026)** and **information bulletin**.

## Can I apply? {#eligibility}

The **Special TET 2026-II** is **only** for in-service teachers working in **Government, Local Body, TGREIS, Government Welfare Residential Societies, Telangana Model Schools** or **other government departments**.

- **Paper I** (Classes I to V): Secondary Grade Teachers and LFL Head Masters in Government and Local Body schools appointed with both D.El.Ed. and B.Ed.
- **Paper II** (Classes VI to VIII): School Assistants, TGTs, Language Pandits and High School Head Masters.
- A teacher of Classes I to V who wants to teach Classes VI to VIII (and has the other qualifications) writes **both papers**.
- A pass in the Special TET counts **only for continuing in your present post or promotion** to the next higher post. It **cannot be used for any direct recruitment**: for that, you need the regular TET or CTET.

## The regular TET {#regular}

To become a teacher through direct recruitment (DSC), you need the **regular TG TET**, held **twice a year** by the State, or **CTET**. Watch **tgtet.aptonline.in** and **schooledu.telangana.gov.in** for its notification; each notification gives the eligibility, dates and fee for that test.

## Documents you will need {#documents}

- **Scanned photograph** (up to 500 KB) and **signature** (up to 100 KB), as separate JPEG files
- Your details for the fee payment: name, date of birth, whether you belong to Telangana, mobile number
- After paying: your **Journal Number**
- For a duplicate hall ticket (if needed): the Journal Number, candidate ID, a printout of your application and the same photograph

## The fee {#fee}

For the Special TET 2026-II: **₹750** for one paper or **₹1,000** for both papers (General and BC); **₹700** for one paper or **₹950** for two papers (SC, ST and PwD). Paid online.

## Steps {#steps}

1. **Download the information bulletin** (free) and check that you are eligible.
2. **Pay the fee online** on **schooledu.telangana.gov.in**, giving your preliminary details. You get a **Journal Number**: this only confirms the fee, not your application.
3. **Fill in the online application** with the Journal Number, following the bulletin. Choose your **Language I** and your **exam district preferences**.
4. **Upload your photograph and signature**, then check that they are clear, the right size and yours. Applications with missing or unclear photos are rejected, and complaints about photo mismatches are not accepted after you submit.
5. **Submit.** You do not need to send a hard copy, but keep a printout.
6. If a **correction window** opens (in 2026-II it ran from 15 to 17 October 2026), you can fix mistakes in the application then.

For the Special TET 2026-II, payment and applications were open from **7 to 16 October 2026**.

## The exam {#cbt}

- A **computer-based test**: each paper has **150 multiple-choice questions**, one mark each, in **2 hours 30 minutes**, with **no negative marking**.
- **Paper I:** Child Development and Pedagogy, Language I, Language II (English), Mathematics, Environmental Studies (30 questions each).
- **Paper II:** Child Development and Pedagogy, Language I and Language II (30 each), and 60 questions in **Mathematics and Science** or **Social Studies**, depending on what you teach.
- **Language I** is one you studied as medium or first language up to Class X (Paper I: Telugu, Urdu, Hindi, Bengali, Kannada, Marathi, Tamil, Gujarati; Paper II: the same without Bengali and Gujarati, plus Sanskrit). Questions are in English and your Language I.
- 2026-II dates: exams **16 to 21 November 2026**, morning (9.00–11.30) and afternoon (2.00–4.30) sessions.

## After you apply {#after}

- **Hall tickets** from **9 November 2026** on schooledu.telangana.gov.in. If you cannot download it, go **in person** to the Director, SCERT, Hyderabad, on a working day (10.30 a.m. to 5 p.m.) with your Journal Number, candidate ID, application printout and photograph. No duplicate is issued after the exam.
- An **initial key** is published after the exam; you can file objections online in the time given. Then the **final key**.
- **Pass marks:** **60%** (General and EWS), **50%** (BC), **40%** (SC, ST and differently abled).
- **Results** between **9 and 11 December 2026**. The Director, SCERT issues the memos and certificates. The Special TET certificate stays valid for life for your present post and promotion.
