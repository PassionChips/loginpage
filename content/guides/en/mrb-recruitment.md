The **Medical Services Recruitment Board (MRB)** recruits staff for Tamil Nadu's government health services: assistant surgeons, nurses, pharmacists, laboratory and other technicians, therapists and more. You apply **only online**. Every recruitment has its own **notification**, which sets the posts, vacancies, eligibility, fee, dates and how candidates are selected. This guide follows MRB's notification for Therapeutic Assistant (Notification No. 02/MRB/2026) and MRB's instructions to candidates; always read the notification for your own post.

## Before you start {#before}

- Find your post on MRB's **Notifications** page and read its notification fully before applying.
- Keep a **mobile number and an e-mail address of your own** active until the results. MRB sends all messages, including calls for certificate verification, **only by SMS to your registered primary mobile number and to your registered e-mail**. If you have no personal e-mail, create one before applying, and do not share it with anyone.
- Keep a **scanned colour photograph** and a **scanned signature** ready.
- Apply and pay **well before the last date**. MRB is not responsible for late submission or payment.

## Can I apply? {#eligibility}

Eligibility is different for each post: qualification, age and other conditions are in the post's notification. **Check that you are eligible before you submit.** MRB says applications with wrong claims or particulars are rejected, and a candidate who gives wrong information can be debarred from future recruitments.

## Documents you will need {#documents}

**For the online application:**

- Your scanned **colour photograph** and **signature**, in the size the notification asks for. An application without them is incomplete.
- Your **community certificate number, issuing authority and date of issue**.
- Scans of the **certificates for every claim** you make in the form (qualification, community, special category and so on). MRB requires them to be uploaded **when you submit**; a claim made later is not considered.

**For certificate verification**, if you are called (the list in MRB's Therapeutic Assistant notification; your notification has the list for your post):

- Proof of date of birth (birth certificate or SSLC)
- Certificates and mark sheets of your educational qualifications
- Proof of Tamil qualification (SSLC or HSC), and the Person Studied in Tamil Medium certificate if you claim it
- Permanent community certificate from the competent authority
- Certificate of character and conduct from a Group A or Group B officer, issued on or after the date of the notification
- Certificate of character and conduct from the head of the institution where you last studied
- Certificates for any special claims: differently abled, destitute widow, ex-serviceman, No Objection Certificate from your employer, equivalence of qualification, transgender ID, gazette for change of name
- The undertaking and declaration in the notification
- Two passport-size colour photographs (the same as the one you uploaded), with your name and application number on the back
- One photo identity card from a government authority (other than PAN card) showing the address you gave

Bring the **originals and one set of attested photocopies**.

## The fee {#fee}

Each notification sets its own fee. For example, Notification No. 02/MRB/2026 charges **₹300** for SC, SCA, ST, differently abled and destitute widow candidates and **₹600** for others. You pay online by **net banking, credit or debit card or mobile wallet**. MRB does not refund the fee, and a registered application cannot be withdrawn.

## Steps {#steps}

1. Open **mrb.tn.gov.in**, go to **Notifications**, and open the notification for your post.
2. Click **Online Registration** on the home page, or the **Online Registration portal link** for your post on the Notifications page, to open the online application form. Select your post.
3. Fill in **every field**. Mobile number and e-mail are mandatory; you can also give an alternate mobile and a landline number.
4. Upload your **photograph and signature**.
5. Enter your **community certificate details** and upload the certificates for every claim.
6. Check every detail and click **SUBMIT**. Write your name and your father's or husband's name exactly as in your certificates. After the last date, no changes are allowed.
7. **Pay the fee** online. After paying, wait for the message from the server: **do not press Back or Refresh**, or you may be charged twice.
8. When the payment succeeds you get a **Registration Number and Password**. Note them down.
9. **Save or print** your application as a PDF. You can download it again later with your registration number and password. Do **not** send the printout or any certificates to MRB.

## After you apply {#after}

- **Selection depends on the post.** Some posts are filled on the marks of your qualifications (for Therapeutic Assistant: Diploma 50%, HSC 30%, SSLC 20%, with no interview); others by a computer based test. Your notification says which.
- MRB publishes each step on its **Notifications** page, under your post: weightage marks lists, admit cards, answer keys with an objection tracker, certificate verification schedules and selection lists. This site lists them on **MRB: what's new**.
- Details of provisionally selected candidates are hosted on MRB's website.

## If you have a computer based test {#cbt}

From MRB's instructions to candidates appearing for a Computer Based Test:

- Write the exam **only at the centre allotted to you**; changes are not allowed.
- Bring your **hall ticket** and an **original photo ID**: Aadhaar card, passport, driving licence, PAN card, voter ID or another valid photo ID. If the photo on your hall ticket is missing, unclear or does not look like you, bring a photo pasted on plain paper with your name, address, register number and signature, with a copy of your hall ticket and ID, for the Chief Invigilator.
- Registration at the venue starts **90 minutes** before the exam. Be at the venue **30 minutes before** the start; nobody is allowed in after that.
- **No electronic devices** are allowed in the hall. Food and drinks are not allowed inside the venue.
- You need **no computer knowledge**: you answer with the mouse. You get a user ID and password at the start; use the keyboard only to type them.
- Questions are in **English and Tamil** (for some posts English only, as the notification says). If in doubt, the English version is final. Each question has four options; you can go back, change or skip answers until the exam closes, and unsubmitted answers are submitted automatically at the end.
- MRB's website has a **mock test** to practise as often as you like.

## Questions and help {#help}

- For problems with online registration, the notification gives a **help desk phone number**.
- Write to MRB only in writing, by post, to the Medical Services Recruitment Board, 7th floor, DMS Buildings, 359 Anna Salai, Teynampet, Chennai 600 006. For a reply, include a stamped addressed envelope.
- MRB does not answer requests for reasons of non-selection, and during a recruitment it gives no information on the selection, including under RTI.

## Good to know

- MRB says all its recruitments are **purely on merit**. Beware of touts and agents who promise jobs: MRB is not responsible for any loss from dealing with them.
- You alone are responsible for what is in your online application, even if a browsing centre or service centre filled it for you. Check it before you submit.
- Use only mrb.tn.gov.in and the registration links on its Notifications page.
