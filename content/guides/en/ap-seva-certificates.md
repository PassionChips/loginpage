**AP Seva** is the Andhra Pradesh government's portal for citizen services of the **Swarna Grama and Swarna Wardu** (village and ward secretariat) system. You apply online, upload your documents and pay a small fee; officers verify the application, and you **download the certificate** from the portal when it is approved. This guide follows the official **"Ease of Living" SOPs** published on the AP Seva portal, which set out the documents, fee, time limit and approving officer for each service.

## Which certificate do you need? {#situation}

- [An income certificate](#income)
- [A caste certificate: the integrated community, nativity and date of birth certificate](#integrated)
- [A birth, death, marriage certificate or another service](#other)

## Before you start {#before}

- You need a **citizen login** on the AP Seva portal.
- Keep **scans of your documents** ready to upload, and a way to **pay online**.
- For some services you may have to give an **undertaking** (a declaration) while applying.
- Keep your **application number**: you use it to track the application.

## Documents you will need {#documents}

**Income certificate:**

- Proof of income: copy of **IT returns** or **pay slips**, or any other income proof
- **Ration card**, **EPIC (voter ID) card** or **Aadhaar card**

**Integrated certificate** (community, nativity and date of birth):

- **SSC marks memo**, **transfer certificate**, or a **date of birth extract** from the municipality or gram panchayat
- A **caste certificate issued earlier** to you, your parents or your family members, wherever applicable

## Income certificate {#income}

- **Approved by:** the Tahsildar or Deputy Tahsildar (Revenue Department)
- **Time limit:** 7 days
- **Fee:** ₹12

1. Open **apseva.ap.gov.in** and click **Citizen Login**.
2. Log in and choose the **Income Certificate** service. Fill in the online application form.
3. Upload your documents, **pay online** and submit.
4. The **Village Revenue Officer (VRO)** checks your details and documents, with a field visit if needed. The **Revenue Inspector (RI)** re-checks the VRO's recommendation, and the **Tahsildar** approves.
5. **Track** the application with your application number.
6. When it is approved, **log in and download or print** the certificate.

## Integrated certificate (community, nativity, date of birth) {#integrated}

This is the certificate Andhra Pradesh issues for **BC, SC, ST and OC** communities, used for scholarships, fee reimbursement and other welfare schemes.

- **Approved by:** the Tahsildar (MRO) or the Revenue Divisional Officer (RDO)
- **Time limit:** 30 days
- **Fee:** ₹12

1. Log in on **apseva.ap.gov.in** and choose the **Integrated Certificate** service. Fill in the form.
2. Upload your documents and submit.
3. The **VRO** verifies, with a field visit if needed, and the **RI** re-checks and sends it to the **Tahsildar**.
4. For some castes the **Tahsildar** approves; for others the Tahsildar recommends and sends it to the **RDO**, who approves or rejects.
5. **Track** where your application is pending on the portal.
6. When it is approved, **download or print** the certificate.

## Other services on AP Seva {#other}

All of these follow the same pattern: log in, choose the service, fill in the form, upload documents, pay, and download the result. From the same SOPs:

| Service | Approved by | Time limit | Fee | Documents |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Birth certificate | Registrar of births and deaths | 7 days | ₹60 | None listed |
| Death certificate | Registrar of births and deaths | 7 days | ₹60 | None listed |
| Hindu marriage certificate | Sub Registrar | 7 days (15 minutes for later copies) | ₹212 (same caste), ₹12 (inter caste) | Wedding card; date of birth proof and residence proof of both; marriage photo |
| New rice card | VRO and MRO | 180 days | ₹24 | Aadhaar numbers of all members; address proof; income certificate; age proof |
| Arogyasri (state health) card | District Arogyasri Coordinator | 20 days | ₹70 | Aadhaar cards; ration card; family photo |
| Encumbrance certificate | Sub Registrar | Same day if applied before 2 p.m., otherwise 3 days | ₹210 | None listed |

## After you apply {#after}

- You get an **SMS** for some services when your application is verified; the approve or reject status is updated on the portal.
- Your **application number** lets you see where the application is pending.
- When it is approved, you **log in and download or print** the certificate from the portal.

## Good to know

- The fees and time limits above are those in the SOPs on the portal; the portal shows the current fee when you apply.
- AP Seva's older address, vswsonline.ap.gov.in, now opens apseva.ap.gov.in.
- Use only the official portal; do not share your login with anyone.
