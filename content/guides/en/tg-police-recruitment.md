The **Telangana State Level Police Recruitment Board (TSLPRB)** recruits constables, sub-inspectors and equivalent posts for the Police, Special Protection Force, Fire and Prisons departments. You apply **only online** on **www.tgprb.in**. This guide follows the four **notifications of 29 July 2026** (Rc Nos. 189, 225, 234 and 279), mainly the **Police Constable (Civil) and equivalent posts** notification, and the **supplementary notification of 15 August 2026**. The board lists **7,437 vacancies** in all.

## Can I apply? {#eligibility}

For the **PC-level posts**:

- **Age:** at least **18** and below **22** years on **1 July 2026**. The Government has raised the upper age limit for uniformed services by **5 years** (G.O.Ms.No.87, 18-05-2026) and by a **further 2 years** (G.O.Ms.No.122, 14-08-2026). SC, ST, BC and EWS candidates get **5 more years**; state government employees, ex-servicemen and NCC instructors have their own relaxations. Home guards with 360 days of duty in two years can apply up to 40 (plus the raises).
- **Education:** **Intermediate** or equivalent, passed by **29 July 2026**.
- **Fire Fighter:** also a valid **LMV driving licence** on the date of notification.
- **Gender:** Constable (Civil), AR, SPF Constable and Fire Fighter are open to men and women; SAR CPL and Warder (Male) only men; Warder (Female) only women.
- **Height:** men at least **167.6 cm**, women at least **152.5 cm** (160 cm and 150 cm for ST and Aboriginal tribe candidates of the listed agency areas).
- **Eyesight and health:** 6/6 distant vision in each eye, full field of vision, no colour blindness or squint, and no defects such as knock-knees, flat feet or varicose veins. The board advises being examined by a Civil Surgeon before applying.

Reservation (BC-A to BC-E, SC Groups I–III, ST, EWS, ex-servicemen, sportspersons, and 33⅓% for women in most posts) applies to **local candidates of Telangana** only.

## Sub Inspector and other SI-level posts {#si}

The SI-level notification follows the same process, with these differences:

- **Age:** at least **21** and below **25** years on 1 July 2026, with the same raises (5 + 2 years) and relaxations.
- **Education:** a **Degree** from a recognised university, by 29 July 2026.
- The written tests and physical events are the same in form; check the SI notification for the papers and marks.

## Documents you will need {#documents}

**Keep ready before you apply:**

- A scanned **passport-size photograph and signature together in one JPG** file, 30 to 100 KB
- **SC community certificate** with the SC sub-classification (Group I, II or III), for SC candidates
- **EWS certificate** issued on or after **1 April 2026**, if applicable
- **Non-creamy layer certificate** issued on or after **1 April 2026**, for BC candidates
- **Local candidature** proof: bonafide certificates from **Class 1 to 7**, or a residence certificate
- Special category certificates (home guard, children of police personnel and so on)

**To upload with the Part II application** (after you pass the preliminary test), and to show in original at certificate verification:

- SSC certificate (date of birth); **only the SSC is accepted for age**
- Intermediate (or Degree, for SI) certificate
- Community certificate, non-creamy layer or EWS certificate, agency area certificate if you claim height relaxation
- Service, NCC, ex-servicemen or sportsperson certificates, and widow / divorce documents, if you claim a relaxation
- Study, bonafide or residence certificates for local status

## Steps {#steps}

1. **Register** on **www.tgprb.in** with **your own mobile number** as your user ID. Keep this number working through the whole recruitment: the board contacts you on it.
2. **Choose the notifications and posts** you are applying for. Submit **one application** for all the posts across the four notifications.
3. **Pay the fee** online (card, net banking or another mode on the site). The fee depends on the posts you choose; SC and ST candidates local to Telangana pay a concessional fee, and there is a rebate for each extra post. **The fee is not refunded.**
4. **Fill Part I** of the application with "Apply Online", upload your photo-and-signature file, read the instructions, click **I AGREE**, and confirm the declaration with the **OTP** sent to your mobile. **No corrections are possible after you submit**, so check every detail.
5. **Download your submitted form** as a PDF.
6. **Preliminary Written Test (PWT):** one 3-hour paper, 200 objective questions, OMR sheet, in English-Telugu and English-Urdu. Wrong answers lose 20% of the question's marks. To qualify you need 30% (OC and EWS), 25% (BC) or 20% (SC, ST, ex-servicemen, home guards). The board's site gives **tentative PWT dates of 29 November (SI-level) and 20 December (PC-level)**.
7. If you pass, **fill Part II** on the site with your post preferences and upload your certificates. Preferences cannot be changed later.
8. **Physical tests** (one session counts for all your posts): **run** 1600 m in 7 min 15 s for men (9 min 30 s for ex-servicemen), 800 m in 5 min 20 s for women; then **height**; then **long jump** (men 4 m, women 2.5 m) and **shot put** (men 6 m with 7.26 kg, women 4 m with 4 kg). There is no postponement.
9. **Final Written Examination:** one 3-hour paper (200 marks for Constable Civil, Fire and Warder; 100 marks for AR, SAR CPL and SPF, where the run also earns marks). To qualify you need 40% (OC), 35% (BC) or 30% (SC, ST, ex-servicemen).
10. **Certificate verification** with originals, then a **medical examination** at the District Headquarters Hospital for those provisionally selected.

## After you apply {#after}

- Selection for Constable (Civil) is on the **Final Written Examination** marks; 95% of the posts in each police district or commissionerate go to local candidates of that unit.
- The board publishes hall tickets, keys, results, cut-offs and selection lists on www.tgprb.in. This site lists them on **TGPRB: what's new**.
- Your candidature stays provisional until certificate verification; false information leads to disqualification and prosecution.

## Good to know

- Exact application dates are announced by the board **through a press release**.
- Fill in the application yourself, or be present if someone fills it for you.
- Impersonation or malpractice means disqualification and criminal prosecution.
