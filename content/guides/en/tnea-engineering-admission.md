**Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions (TNEA)** is the single window for first-year **B.E. and B.Tech** admission to Government and Government-aided engineering colleges, the university departments and constituent colleges of Anna University, Annamalai University, and the seats that self-financing colleges surrender to the government. You register **one online application** for all of them. This guide follows the official **TNEA 2026 Information Brochure** ("Information and Instructions to Candidates"); the rules, fees and dates are set again every year, so check the brochure for your year on tneaonline.org.

## Can I apply? {#eligibility}

You must meet these conditions **on the last date for the online application**.

**Nativity** (who can apply), as the brochure lists it:

- Students who passed VIII, IX, X, XI and XII standard in schools in Tamil Nadu. They need no nativity certificate.
- Tamil Nadu natives who studied any of these classes outside Tamil Nadu, with a **nativity certificate in electronic form** (digitally signed e-certificate).
- Children of Central Government, public sector or recognised institution employees serving in Tamil Nadu for the past five years, with a certificate from the employer; children of All India Service officers of the Tamil Nadu cadre, with a self-declaration from the parent; and students from other states who studied VIII to XII in Tamil Nadu.

**Marks**: a pass in HSC (+1 and +2) with at least this **average in Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry in +2**:

| Community | Minimum average |
| --- | --- |
| General | 45% |
| Backward Class, including Backward Class Muslim | 40% |
| MBC & DNC | 40% |
| SC / SCA / ST | 40% |

Students of the HSC vocational stream are eligible with the same percentages in the related subject, vocational theory and practicals; the brochure lists the vocational subjects.

## Documents you will need {#documents}

**To register**, keep these ready:

- Mobile phone number and e-mail ID
- 10th mark sheet and +2 mark sheet
- Details of the schools you studied in from 6th to 12th standard
- Community / caste details
- Aadhaar number
- Your parents' annual income
- Credit or debit card, net banking, UPI or demand draft details for the fee

**To upload later** (TNEA gives the instructions after registration), copies of the originals:

- 10th mark sheet, +1 mark sheet and +2 (or equivalent) mark sheet
- Transfer certificate
- For the 7.5% reservation for Government school students: details of the Government schools you studied in from 6th to 12th
- Permanent community certificate (card or digitally signed e-certificate) for ST, SC, SCA, MBC & DNC, BC and BCM
- Nativity certificate, only as an e-certificate (if it applies to you)
- Income certificate (if it applies)
- First Graduate certificate (e-certificate) and the First Graduate joint declaration (if you claim it)
- Sri Lankan Tamil refugee certificate (if it applies)
- For special reservation: ex-servicemen certificates with the parent's discharge certificate and ID card; a medical certificate from a Medical Board of three doctors for differently abled candidates; sports achievement certificates for eminent sportspersons

## The fee {#fee}

The 2026 registration fee was **₹500** for OC, BC, BCM and MBC & DNC candidates and **₹250** for SC, SCA and ST candidates, paid online or by demand draft.

## Steps {#steps}

1. Open **tneaonline.org** (or dte.tn.gov.in) and **register** with a username and password. Remember them: you use the same login for the application and for every round of counselling. TNEA Facilitation Centres (TFC) in selected cities can help you.
2. **Fill in the application.** Choose whether you apply for a special reservation (7.5% for Government school students, sons and daughters of ex-servicemen, differently abled persons, eminent sportspersons), and whether you want the First Graduate fee concession or the post-matric scholarship.
3. **Check your entries.** You can view and edit them with your login. When everything is correct, **submit on or before the last date**. You get a PDF of your application.
4. **Pay the registration fee.**
5. **Upload your certificates** when TNEA asks, following its instructions.

## Rank list {#rank}

- Your **merit mark** is out of **200**: Mathematics (100), Physics (50) and Chemistry (50), from your +2 marks. Ties are broken as the brochure explains.
- TNEA publishes the **overall rank and community rank** on its portal. If you have a grievance about your rank, contact the nearest TFC **within 5 days** of the rank list.

## Online counselling {#counselling}

Counselling is **online, in several rounds**; you take part in the round for your rank, using the same login. Each round has four stages:

1. **Choice filling**: you get **three days** to fill in your choices of colleges and branches. You can fill any number, and **the order matters**.
2. **Tentative allotment**, by your rank, your order of choices and the seats available.
3. **Confirmation**, within **two days** of the allotment, with one of these options:
   - **Accept and Join**: take the seat.
   - **Accept and Upward**: keep the seat but wait for a higher choice; if none comes, you keep this seat.
   - **Decline and Upward**: refuse the seat and wait for a higher choice; if none comes, you move to the next round.
   - **Decline and move to next round**.
   - **Decline and Quit**: leave counselling.
   - If no seat was allotted: **Upward or move to next round**.
   If you do not confirm in time, you are moved to the next round.
4. **Reporting**:
   - After **Accept and Join**, download the allotment order and **report to the college within 5 days**, paying the fees. If you do not, the allotment is cancelled.
   - After **Accept and Upward**, report to the nearest **TFC**, pay the tuition fee and hand over your original certificates to hold the seat.
   - Students allotted a seat under the Government school 7.5% quota, and those who uploaded the certificates for the First Graduate concession or the post-matric scholarship, do not pay the fee there.

## Fee concessions {#concessions}

The brochure describes, among others:

- **First Graduate tuition fee concession**, for a student who would be the family's first graduate; not available if a brother or sister already got it for a professional course.
- **AICTE Tuition Fee Waiver**, in self-financing colleges and self-supporting courses, for students whose parents' annual income is below ₹8 lakh.
- **Post-matric scholarship** for SC, SCA and ST students (and SC/SCA converted Christians) whose parents' annual income is below ₹2.5 lakh, claimed through the college.

## Good to know

- Do all counselling steps (adding and locking choices, allotment, your confirmation) only on the official portals, tneaonline.org or dte.tn.gov.in.
- The brochure for the current year is the final word on eligibility, fees, dates and documents.
