The law says your PAN must be linked to your Aadhaar. If it is not linked, your PAN can become **inoperative**. Then you cannot get tax refunds, and tax may be cut from your income at a higher rate. Linking it again makes the PAN work again.

You can do the whole thing yourself on the official portal, in three parts: pay the fee, wait a few days, then link.

## Documents you will need {#documents}

- **Your PAN** (the 10-character number on your PAN card).
- **Your Aadhaar number** (12 digits).
- **A mobile phone** that can receive an OTP.
- **Net banking, a debit card or UPI** to pay the fee.

### Who does not need to link

The portal's Link Aadhaar page lists these exempted groups (Department of Revenue Notification 37/2017):

- Non-resident Indians (NRIs)
- People who are not citizens of India
- People who were more than 80 years old at any time during the previous year
- People whose state of residence is Assam, Meghalaya or Jammu & Kashmir

### The fee

If you link after the due date you must pay a fee **before** you can link. The Income Tax Department's FAQ says the fee is **₹1,000** (Rule 114(5A)). You do not type the amount yourself: the portal fills it in for you.

## Part 1: Pay the fee {#pay}

1. Open the [Income Tax e-Filing portal](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/). Under **Quick Links**, click **Link Aadhaar/View Status**.

   ![The Quick Links on the e-Filing home page, with "Link Aadhaar/View Status" outlined in red](/static/guides/pan-aadhaar-link/1-quick-links.webp "Screenshot of incometax.gov.in, 14 Oct 2026. The red outline is ours.")

2. On the **Link Aadhaar/View Status** page, click **Continue** under **Link Aadhaar**.

   ![The Link Aadhaar/View Status page with two boxes: Link Aadhaar and View Status](/static/guides/pan-aadhaar-link/2-landing.webp "Screenshot of incometax.gov.in, 14 Oct 2026.")

3. Type your **PAN** and **Aadhaar number** and click **Validate**.

   ![The Link Aadhaar form with boxes for PAN and Aadhaar Number, and a note listing who is exempt](/static/guides/pan-aadhaar-link/3-form.webp "Screenshot of incometax.gov.in, 14 Oct 2026.")

4. If you have not paid yet, the portal says it could not find your payment. Click **Continue to Pay Through e-Pay Tax**.
5. Type your PAN again, confirm it, and enter your mobile number. Type the OTP you get by SMS.
6. On the e-Pay Tax page, click **Proceed** on the **Income Tax** box.
7. Choose the **Tax Year** the portal shows. For **Type of Payment** choose **Other Receipts (500)**. For **Sub type of Payment** choose **Fee for delay in Linking Pan with Aadhaar**. Click **Continue**.
8. The amount is already filled in. Click **Continue**, choose how you want to pay (net banking, debit card, UPI and so on) and pay.
9. Save the challan receipt the portal gives you.

**Wait 4 to 5 working days** after paying. The portal needs that time to see your payment.

## Part 2: Link PAN and Aadhaar {#link}

1. Go back to the portal and click **Link Aadhaar/View Status**, then **Continue** under **Link Aadhaar**, as in Part 1.
2. Type your PAN and Aadhaar number and click **Validate**.
3. A message says your payment details are verified. Click **Continue**.
4. Fill in the details the form asks for, including a mobile number, and click **Link Aadhaar**.
5. Type the 6-digit OTP sent to that mobile number and click **Validate**.
6. The portal shows: *"Request for link of Aadhaar has been submitted successfully, now you can check the Aadhaar-PAN link status."* UIDAI (the Aadhaar authority) then checks the request.

## Part 3: Check that it worked {#status}

1. On the portal home page, click **Link Aadhaar/View Status**, then **Continue** under **View Status**.
2. Type your PAN and Aadhaar number and click **View Link Aadhaar Status**.
3. If the link is done, the portal shows that your PAN is linked to your Aadhaar. If it says the request is still pending with UIDAI, check again later. If it failed, click **Link Aadhaar** and do Part 2 again.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Payment details not found"** after you paid: wait 4 to 5 working days and try again.
- **PAN already linked to a different Aadhaar:** only the Income Tax Department can fix this. Contact your Jurisdictional Assessing Officer (the portal's **Know Your JAO** link on the home page shows who that is).
- **Make sure you type your own, correct Aadhaar number.** Undoing a link to the wrong Aadhaar needs your Assessing Officer.
- **Help:** the e-Filing helpdesk is on 1800 103 0025 and 1800 419 0025 (8 am to 8 pm, Monday to Friday).

## Good to know

- If you get a new PAN today, the portal links it to your Aadhaar during the application.
- Use only incometax.gov.in. The Income Tax Department says its official emails **never contain links**. If an email or message asks you to click a link to "link PAN", treat it as a scam.
