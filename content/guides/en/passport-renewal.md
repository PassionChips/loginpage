In India, "renewing" a passport is officially called **Re-issue of Passport**. You apply for a re-issue when:

- your passport has **expired**, or will **expire soon**
- its **pages are full**
- it is **damaged**, **lost** or **stolen**
- you want to **change your details** (name, address, spouse's name and so on)

There are no screenshots on this page. The Passport Seva website does not allow its content to be copied without the Ministry of External Affairs' permission.

## Documents you will need {#documents}

Take the **originals** and **one set of self-attested photocopies** to the Passport Seva Kendra. This is the list the portal's Document Advisor gave on the date above for an adult, Normal (not Tatkaal) application, with a passport that has expired:

- **Your old passport, in original**, with self-attested photocopies of:
  - its first two and last two pages
  - the ECR/Non-ECR page
  - the page of observation, if there is one
  - the validity extension page, if any
- **Proof of your present address**: needed **only if your address is different** from the one in your old passport. The portal's "List of Acceptable Documents" shows what is accepted (for example Aadhaar, an electricity, water or phone bill, a rent agreement and others).

Other cases need other documents. The portal lists these:

- **Adding your spouse's name:** marriage certificate, **or** a Joint Photo Declaration signed by husband and wife (Annexure J).
- **Removing your spouse's name:** divorce order or decree.
- **Changing your spouse's name:** divorce order/decree or death certificate of the first spouse, **and** re-marriage certificate or Annexure J.
- **Women changing surname after marriage or divorce:** marriage certificate or Annexure J; for divorce, the divorce order or decree.
- **Lost or damaged passport:** the portal provides a specimen affidavit (Annexure F).

Your case may be different: Tatkaal, minors, government employees and other situations add documents. Before you apply, run the [Document Advisor for re-issue](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/psp/docAdvisor/reissuePassport) with your own answers. The portal also asks you to check your **Regional Passport Office's page** for any extra documents.

## The fee {#fee}

Fees were revised on **1 July 2026**. Use the portal's **Fee Calculator** to see the current amount for your case. You pay online before you can book an appointment.

## Steps {#steps}

1. **Register** on the [Passport Seva portal](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/psp): click **Register**, fill in your details and click **Sign Up**. Confirm your email address, then **log in**.
   - Your password expires every 3 months. Reset it when the portal asks.
   - You choose a **Regional Passport Office (RPO)** for where you live now. To re-issue from a different RPO than the one that issued your old passport, create a **new login ID** for that RPO. You can use the same email address.
2. On your dashboard, click **Services**, then the **Apply for Fresh Passport/Re-issue of Passport** card. Choose **re-issue**.
   - If you have ever held an ordinary (dark blue) passport, choose **re-issue**, not fresh.
3. **Fill in the form.** It has these steps: Passport Type, Applicant Details, Family Details, Address Details, Emergency Contact, Previous Passport, Other Details, Passport Preview, Details Verification.
   - You can save and come back later.
   - **Check everything before you submit.** You cannot change the application after submitting it.
4. **Submit.** You get an **Application Reference Number (ARN)**.
5. **Pay and book an appointment.** Click **View**, then **Pay and Schedule Appointment**.
   - Choose the appointment quota (Normal or Tatkaal) and the Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) or Post Office Passport Seva Kendra (POPSK) you want.
   - Pay by credit or debit card, internet banking or UPI.
   - You can reschedule up to **three times** (Tatkaal: once).
6. You get an **SMS** with your appointment details. A printed receipt is **no longer required**; the SMS is accepted as proof of your appointment.
7. **Visit the PSK on your appointment day** with your original documents and their photocopies.

## Good to know

- **Visit within 90 days.** If you do not visit the PSK within 90 days of submitting the form online, you must submit a new application.
- Only emergency or medical cases and pre-approved categories can visit without an appointment.
- The Document Advisor's list is a guide only: the portal says the decision of the Passport Officer (APO/RPO) is final.
- National Call Center: **1800-258-1800**.
- Use only passportindia.gov.in. Be careful with look-alike websites and agents who ask for extra money.
