**Instant e-PAN** is the Income Tax Department's free way to get a new PAN. You get your PAN as a PDF file (an e-PAN). The portal says it **holds the same value as a physical PAN card**.

## Can I use this? {#eligibility}

You can, if **all** of these are true:

- You are an individual (a person, not a company or firm).
- You **do not already have a PAN**. Having two PANs is against the law, and the portal warns that it can attract a **penalty of ₹10,000**.
- You are **not a minor** (under 18) on the day you apply.
- You have an **Aadhaar** number, and a **mobile number linked to that Aadhaar**.
- You have a **DigiLocker** account, and it holds one of the documents in the next section as proof of your date of birth.
- You are not applying as a representative of someone else (a "representative assessee").

If you cannot use Instant e-PAN, you can still apply for a PAN through the other official channels, Protean (formerly NSDL) or UTIITSL. The portal also sends you there if your e-PAN request is rejected or not handled within 24 hours.

## Documents you will need {#documents}

No papers to upload. You need:

- **Aadhaar number**, with the **mobile number linked to it** nearby (you will get two OTPs).
- **DigiLocker security PIN** (the 6-digit PIN you set in DigiLocker).
- In your DigiLocker, **one** of these as proof of date of birth:
  - Birth certificate
  - Matriculation (Class 10) certificate
  - Matriculation marksheet from a recognised board
  - Driving licence
  - CGHS card
- **Your father's name**, which you type in.

## Cost

**Free.** The portal says the PAN "is issued in PDF format to applicants, which is free of cost".

## Steps {#steps}

1. Open the [Income Tax e-Filing portal](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/). Under **Quick Links**, click **Instant E-PAN**.

   ![The Quick Links on the e-Filing home page, with "Instant E-PAN" outlined in red](/static/guides/pan-instant-epan/1-quick-links.webp "Screenshot of incometax.gov.in, 14 Oct 2026. The red outline is ours.")

2. On the e-PAN page, click **Get New e-PAN**.

   ![The e-PAN page with two boxes, "Get New e-PAN" and "Check Status/ Download e-PAN"; the Get New e-PAN button is outlined](/static/guides/pan-instant-epan/2-epan-page.webp "Screenshot of incometax.gov.in, 14 Oct 2026. The red outline is ours.")

3. Tick all four eligibility statements and the box that lets the portal use your DigiLocker and Aadhaar e-KYC data. Click **Continue**.
4. Read the disclaimer and click **Continue**. The portal takes you to **DigiLocker**.
5. In DigiLocker, type your **Aadhaar number** (or Virtual ID) and the captcha, and click **Next**.
6. Type the **OTP** sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile and click **Continue**.
7. Type your **6-digit DigiLocker security PIN** and click **Continue**. Forgot it? Use **Forgot security PIN?**
8. Choose the document to share as proof of your date of birth, and click **Allow**. (If you choose more than one, the first one in alphabetical order is used.)
9. Back on the e-Filing portal, type your Aadhaar number again and ask for an OTP.
10. Type the new OTP and click **Continue**. Each OTP works for **15 minutes**, and you get **3 tries**.
11. Type your **father's name**, tick **I Accept that**, click **Submit**, then **Continue**.
12. Adding your email is optional. Write down the **Acknowledgement Number** shown on the screen. You will also get an SMS.

## Download your e-PAN {#download}

1. Go to the same **Instant E-PAN** page and click **Continue** on **Check Status/ Download e-PAN**.
2. Type your Aadhaar number and click **Continue**.
3. Type the OTP and click **Validate Aadhaar OTP and Continue**.
4. If your PAN has been allotted, click **Download e-PAN** (or **View e-PAN**).

After you get your PAN you can also register on the e-Filing portal (the page shows a **Create e-Filing Account** button), which you will need for tax returns.

## Help

- e-Filing helpdesk: **1800 103 0025** or **1800 419 0025** (8 am to 8 pm, Monday to Friday).
- PAN queries (Protean, formerly NSDL): **+91-20-27218080** (7 am to 11 pm, all days).

## Good to know

- Use only the official incometax.gov.in portal. It is free there, so there is no need to pay an agent for this service.
- The Income Tax Department says its official emails **never contain links**. Treat any message with a "get PAN" link as a scam.
