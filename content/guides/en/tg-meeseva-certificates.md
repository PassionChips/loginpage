**MeeSeva** is Telangana's single window for government services: revenue certificates, birth and death certificates, ration cards, electoral roll changes, electricity connections, licences and more. You apply at a **MeeSeva centre** or with a **citizen login** on the MeeSeva portal; the department concerned verifies the application and the certificate is issued through MeeSeva. This guide follows the official **MeeSeva application forms** on the Telangana State Portal (telangana.gov.in, "MeeSeva Services"), which list the documents for each service, and the **MeeSeva citizen charter** fixed by the Commissioner, ESD (ITE&C Department), which sets the time and charge for each service.

## Which certificate do you need? {#situation}

- [An income certificate](#income)
- [A caste certificate: the integrated community and date of birth certificate](#integrated)
- [A residence certificate](#residence)
- [An EWS, non-creamy layer or other service](#other)

## Before you start {#before}

- **Download the application form** for your service from the State Portal's MeeSeva Services page, or ask at the MeeSeva centre. Some forms are in Telugu, some in English.
- In the forms, documents marked **\*** are **compulsory**, and from those marked **#** you must attach **at least one**.
- Keep your **landline, mobile number and e-mail** ready: the forms ask for them.
- Some services in the citizen charter are **instant (about 15 minutes)**, because the record already exists; others need **verification** by the department and take days.

## Documents you will need {#documents}

**Income certificate:**

- Application form (**not needed if you apply with a citizen login**)
- **Ration card, Aadhaar card or EPIC (voter ID) card** (compulsory)
- **Copy of IT returns, pay slips or other proof of income, or an affidavit**
- **Passport-size photo** (compulsory)

**Integrated certificate** (caste / community and date of birth, for SC and BC; separate forms exist for ST and BC-E):

- Application form (Form 2, to the Tahsildar) (compulsory)
- At least one of each: a **caste certificate already issued** to a family member or relative; the **school transfer certificate** or **Class 10 marks memo**; **study certificates for Classes 1 to 10** or a date of birth certificate; the form's **Schedules 1 to 4** (family tree, spouse's family, property, children); **ration card, Aadhaar card or voter ID**

**Residence certificate:**

- Application form (compulsory)
- **Ration card, Aadhaar number or voter ID** (at least one)
- **House tax receipt, electricity bill or telephone bill** (at least one)

## Income certificate {#income}

- **Citizen charter:** issued in **7 days**, charge **₹45 plus postal charges**.

1. Go to a **MeeSeva centre** with your documents, or log in on the MeeSeva portal (with a citizen login you do not need the paper form).
2. Give your details and the purpose of the certificate, and hand over or upload the documents.
3. **Pay the charge** and keep the **receipt / application number**.
4. The **Revenue department** verifies the application and issues the certificate.
5. **Collect or download** the certificate through MeeSeva, and track the application with your number.

## Integrated certificate (caste) {#integrated}

- **Citizen charter:** issued in **30 days**, charge **₹45**.
- The form is a **declaration to the Tahsildar**: it asks your parents' caste and sub-caste, religion, the permanent address of your parents or grandparents on the date the caste was first notified, your place and date of birth, and whether you are adopted.
- False information is punishable under the Telangana (SC, ST and BC) Act 16 of 1993.

1. Fill in **Form 2** and the four schedules; sign it, with your parent or guardian.
2. Submit it at a **MeeSeva centre** with the documents and pay the charge.
3. The application goes to the **Tahsildar** for verification and issue. Track it with your application number.

## Residence certificate {#residence}

- **Citizen charter:** **15 minutes** where the record is already available, otherwise **7 days**; charge **₹45 plus postal charges**. There is a general residence certificate and a separate one for **passport** purposes.
- In the form you state how many years you have lived at your address, and the purpose.

## Other services {#other}

| Service | Citizen charter | Form / note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Family member certificate | 15 min, ₹45 + postal | "FAMC" form |
| Agricultural income certificate | 15 min or 7 days, ₹45 | Revenue form |
| Nativity certificate (BC / SC / ST) | 30 days, ₹45 | Revenue form |
| No earning member certificate | 7 days, ₹45 | Revenue form |
| Birth / death certificate (municipalities, CDMA) | 15 min (₹35) or 5 days (₹45) | CDMA forms; GHMC has its own |
| EWS certificate | as per the charter | Form with family **assets** (land, flats, plots) and **income** from all sources; self-attested photo |
| Non-creamy layer certificate (BC-A to E) | as per the charter | **Form VII-A** to the Tahsildar, with your parents' posts, income and property |

The State Portal's MeeSeva Services page also has forms for **ration cards** (new pink card, member addition or deletion, transfer), **voter list** changes (Form 6, 8, 8A), **electricity** connections, **employment exchange** registration, the **SADAREM** disability certificate, **encumbrance** certificates, scholarships and many licences.

## After you apply {#after}

- Keep your **MeeSeva receipt**: it has the application number to track the status.
- Services that need verification go to the department concerned: the integrated (caste) and non-creamy layer forms are addressed to the **Tahsildar**.
- Certificates for jobs often have to be **recent**: for example, the 2026 police recruitment asks for EWS and non-creamy layer certificates issued on or after 1 April 2026. Check the notification before you apply.

## Good to know

- The charges above are from the MeeSeva citizen charter; ask the centre for the current charge.
- The official MeeSeva portal is ts.meeseva.telangana.gov.in; the application forms are on telangana.gov.in under Services, MeeSeva Services.
