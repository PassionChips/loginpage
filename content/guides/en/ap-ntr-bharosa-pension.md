The **NTR Bharosa Pension Scheme** is Andhra Pradesh's monthly social security pension for old people, widows, single women, people with disabilities and some traditional occupations. **SERP** (Society for Elimination of Rural Poverty) runs it. You apply **at your Swarna Grama (village) or Swarna Wardu (ward) office**, not online by yourself. This guide follows the Government's guidelines for new pension applications, **Memo No. 3407491/RD.I/A1/2026 dated 06.09.2026**, and the scheme's official website.

## Who can get a pension {#eligibility}

The categories, with the monthly amounts the scheme's website gives under **G.O.Ms.No.43 dated 13.06.2024**:

| Category | Pension a month |
| --- | --- |
| Old age, widow, single women, weavers, fishermen, toddy tappers, traditional cobblers, dappu artists, transgender persons, ART (PLHIV) persons | ₹4,000 |
| Persons with disabilities, multi-deformity leprosy | ₹6,000 |
| Fully disabled persons; listed chronic diseases (for example kidney, liver and heart transplant, chronic kidney disease, people on dialysis) | ₹10,000 or more: check the current amount on the website |

The website's English text gives ₹10,000 for fully disabled persons, while its Telugu notice says ₹15,000; ask at your Swarna Grama or Swarna Wardu office for the current amount.

- Each application is checked against the scheme's **common eligibility criteria** (G.O.Ms.No.174, 2019) and against the **list of existing pensioners**.
- **New pensions are sanctioned category by category, in phases.** The Government opens an application window: in 2026 the offices received new applications from **7 to 16 September 2026**. Ask your office when the next window opens.

## Documents you will need {#documents}

**For every category:**

- **Aadhaar card**
- **Rice card** or **income certificate**

**And for your category:**

- **Old age:** Aadhaar card as proof of age
- **Widow:** husband's death certificate and husband's Aadhaar number
- **Single women:** if married, the divorce decree from a court or Lok Adalat; if unmarried, a non-marriage certificate from the Tahsildar
- **Disabled:** **SADAREM certificate** showing a benchmark disability of **40% or more**
- **Weavers:** certificate from the Handlooms & Textiles Department
- **Fishermen:** certificate from the Fisheries Department
- **Dappu artists** and **traditional cobblers:** certificate from the Social Welfare Department
- **Toddy tappers:** certificate from the Prohibition & Excise Department
- **Transgender persons:** certificate from the District Medical Board

## Steps {#steps}

1. **Get the application form** ("Application Form for New Pension") at your Swarna Grama or Swarna Wardu office, or download it from **sspensions.ap.gov.in**. It is in Telugu.
2. **Fill it in:** your name exactly as in Aadhaar, Aadhaar number, father's or husband's name and Aadhaar number (required for a widow pension), gender, date of birth and age, marital status, caste, mobile number, full address with your Swarna Grama / Swarna Wardu name, mandal or municipality, district and PIN code, and the **type of pension**. Tick the documents you attach, then sign or give your thumb impression on the declaration. A photo is optional.
3. **Submit it at your Swarna Grama or Swarna Wardu office** with the documents. The Digital Assistant or Ward Education and Data Processing Secretary registers it online and gives you a **system-generated acknowledgement**. Keep it.
4. **eKYC:** your details are checked against your Aadhaar eKYC. If something does not match, you are told what to correct and asked to bring the right details.
5. **Eligibility check:** your application is checked against the common eligibility rules and the existing pensioners list. If it is rejected here, you get an **endorsement with the reasons**.
6. **Home visit:** office staff visit your home to verify your eligibility and documents. If your eKYC was not done when you applied, they do it during this visit.
7. **Scrutiny and recommendation:** the MPDO (rural) or Municipal Commissioner (urban) checks everything and recommends eligible applications. Single women's applications are first verified by the **Tahsildar**; occupational pensions (weavers, fishermen, dappu artists, cobblers, toddy tappers, transgender persons) by the concerned **district-level department**. The MPDO only recommends; the sanction comes from the state level.
8. **Sanction:** the state departments and SERP verify, the Government approves the budget, and a **sanction order** is generated. The Welfare & Education Assistant or Ward Welfare & Development Secretary hands it to you.

## After you apply {#after}

- The pension is paid **from the month SERP sanctions and releases it**. It is **not paid backwards** from the date you applied or became eligible.
- If your application is found ineligible at any stage, you receive an endorsement with the reasons from your office.
- The 2026 timeline was short: about two weeks from receipt to the budget proposal (7 to 26 September 2026).

## Questions and help {#help}

- Ask first at your **Swarna Grama or Swarna Wardu office**.
- Social Security Pensions (SERP), 2nd floor, Dr. N.T.R. Administrative Block, Pandit Nehru RTC Bus Complex, Vijayawada 520001. Phone **0866-2410017**.

## Good to know

- The Government's guidelines make the Swarna Grama and Swarna Wardu offices the place to apply; the form can also be downloaded from the scheme's website.
- Keep your acknowledgement, any endorsement and the sanction order: they show where your application stands.
