The **Teachers Recruitment Board (TRB)** recruits **Assistant Professors** for the Tamil Nadu Collegiate Educational Service, for **Government Arts & Science Colleges** and **Government Colleges of Education**. You apply only online. This guide follows TRB's own instructions to candidates, "How to Apply through Online". Always read the recruitment notification on the TRB website for the posts, dates and fees of the current recruitment.

## Before you start {#before}

- Use an **e-mail address and mobile number that you use now and will keep active**. TRB sends messages only to the registered e-mail and mobile number. If you do not have them, create an e-mail address and get a mobile number first.
- Fill in **every field marked with a star (⋆)**. These are mandatory.

## Can I apply? {#eligibility}

TRB's instructions list these general conditions. The recruitment notification has the full eligibility for the posts.

- Your **Post Graduate degree** must be from a recognised university, under the **10 + 2 + 3 + 2** pattern. Only these candidates are considered for selection (G.O.(Ms) No. 107, P & AR Department, dated 18.08.2009).
- You are **not eligible** if you got a UG or PG degree after a course of **one year** (a double degree or dual degree).
- You are **not eligible** if you got any of the required qualifications **at the same time** as another: SSLC, HSC or equivalent, UG degree, PG degree, B.Ed and M.Ed.

## Documents you will need {#documents}

You upload scans of these in the application's **Uploading Document** page:

- Your **photo** and **signature**
- Proof of your **date of birth**
- Your **community certificate**
- Your **qualification** certificates
- Any other documents for the **personal details and claims** you make in the form

Make sure every scan is **clear**. TRB says unclear documents are not scrutinised and are **summarily rejected**, and that failing to upload the documents for a claim you make in the form leads to **rejection of your candidature**. Upload each document in its own place.

## Steps {#steps}

### 1. Sign up as a new user

1. On the TRB application site, open **New User Sign up** and fill in your basic personal details.
2. Enter your e-mail address and **validate it with the OTP** sent to it.
3. **Validate your mobile number** with the OTP sent to it.
4. Fill in all mandatory details and click **SUBMIT**.
5. Your **User ID and Password** are shown on the screen and sent to your e-mail and mobile. Keep them safe.
6. Go to the **Login** page, enter your User ID and Password, and click **Login**.

### 2. Fill in the application

The application form has these sections, in this order:

1. Important dates
2. Details of the Applicant
3. Address
4. Special Category details
5. Employment details
6. Research Experience
7. Educational Qualification
8. Examination particulars
9. Uploading Document
10. Preview and Confirmation

In **Details of the Applicant**:

- Enter your **Nativity** and **Gender**. When you choose the gender, a confirmation box opens; click **OK** to confirm it.
- Fill in your **Permanent Address** and **Communication Address**.
- Choose your **Community** from the drop-down, fill in your **Religion** and community details, and **upload your community certificate**.
- Click **Save & Continue**, then fill in your **Educational Qualification** details.

### 3. Upload your documents

In **Uploading Document**, upload each document listed above in its own space. Check that each one is clear before you move on.

### 4. Preview, submit and download

1. **Preview** shows everything you entered, with an **Edit** button for each section. Use it to correct or update anything.
2. At the end of the preview, click **Proceed to Submit Form**.
3. Click **Submit Application Form**. A **Declaration** box opens: tick the checkbox and click **Submit**.
4. In your **Applicant's Dashboard**, click **APPLICATION FORM** to download your filled-in application. Keep a copy.

## After you submit {#after}

- **You cannot change anything** in the application once you click Submit. Check the preview carefully first.
- Applying and writing the examination **does not give you a right to be selected**.
- TRB can **cancel or postpone the examination** at any time, on administrative and other grounds.
- TRB is not responsible for **late or incomplete** submissions. Do not leave it to the last day.

## Questions and help {#help}

Send any query **only by e-mail, from the e-mail address you registered with**, to **trbgrievances@tn.gov.in**. TRB does not accept queries by post, by letter, or in person.

## Good to know

- Use only the official TRB website, trb.tn.gov.in.
- The recruitment notification decides the posts, the dates, the fee and the full eligibility. This guide covers how the online application works.
