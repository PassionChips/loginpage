Telangana recruits teachers for **Government and Local Body schools** through the **District Selection Committee (DSC)**, run by the **Commissioner of School Education**. The latest recruitment on the official site is **DSC-2024**, which notified **11,062 posts** (Notification 24/RC-1/TRT/DSC/2024, 29-02-2024); its general ranking lists have been published. **There is no newer DSC notification yet.** This guide explains how the recruitment works, from the official **DSC-2024 information bulletin**, so you can prepare; every new DSC has its own notification and bulletin with that year's posts, dates and rules.

## Which posts? {#posts}

DSC recruits **School Assistants** (Mathematics, Physical Science, Biological Science, Social Studies, English, Telugu, Hindi, Urdu and other languages), **Secondary Grade Teachers (SGT)**, **Language Pandits**, **Physical Education Teachers (PET)**, and **Special Education teachers** at primary (SGT) and upper primary / secondary (School Assistant) level. Vacancies are notified **district-wise, category-wise, subject-wise and medium-wise**.

## Can I apply? {#eligibility}

The qualifications are set in the **Teacher Recruitment Rules** (G.O.Ms.No.25, School Education, 05-09-2023, as amended). From the DSC-2024 bulletin:

- **Secondary Grade Teacher:** Intermediate with at least **50%** (45% for SC, ST, BC and differently abled), or 45% / 40% under the NCTE 2002 norms, **and** a 2-year **D.El.Ed.** (or Special Education D.Ed., or 4-year B.El.Ed.), **and** a pass in **Paper I** of TG TET, APTET or CTET.
- **School Assistant:** a **degree** (or PG) in the subject with at least **50%** (45% for SC, ST, BC and differently abled) and a **B.Ed.** with that subject as a methodology (or a 4-year integrated B.A.B.Ed. / B.Sc.B.Ed.), **and** a pass in **Paper II** of TG TET, APTET or CTET with the right optional.
- Final-year students of the required qualification could apply in 2024, if they qualified by the date of certificate verification.
- Degrees through open or distance universities count only if recognised by the UGC / AICTE / Distance Education Bureau.
- **Age (DSC-2024):** **18 to 46** years on 1 July 2023, with relaxations: Telangana government employees up to 5 years, ex-servicemen 3 years plus service, SC / ST / BC / EWS 5 years, physically handicapped 10 years.

**TET matters twice:** you must have passed TET for the post, and TET marks count for **20%** of the selection marks.

## Documents you will need {#documents}

For the **certificate verification** checklist of DSC-2024, keep originals and copies of:

- SSC (Class 10) certificate, for date of birth
- Intermediate (10+2), degree and post-graduation marks memos and certificates
- Professional qualification (D.El.Ed. / B.Ed. and so on), with its methodology subjects
- TET pass certificate and marks memo
- Community / caste certificate (number and date of issue)
- Study certificates for **Classes I to VII** (school names and years), for local status
- EWS certificate, SADAREM certificate (40% or more disability), ex-servicemen certificate, sports certificates, if you claim them
- For Telangana government employees: an **NOC** from your department
- A passport-size photograph, attested by the DEO at verification

## Steps {#steps}

1. **Read the notification and information bulletin** for your post on the DSC website, and check the district-wise vacancies.
2. **Pay the fee** online: in DSC-2024 it was **₹1,000 for each post**. You get a **Payment Reference ID**; it only confirms payment.
3. **Fill in the online application** with the Payment Reference ID, following the user guide. Choose your **test centre district** preferences.
4. **Upload your scanned photo and signature** and submit. Print your filled-in application.
5. If you are already in government service, **inform your Head of Office in writing** that you have applied.
6. **Download your hall ticket** and write the **Computer Based Test**. For SGT, the test is 160 questions for 80 marks in 2 hours 30 minutes: general knowledge, perspectives in education, two languages, mathematics, science, social studies and teaching methodology. The site has a **mock test**.
7. Check the **initial key**, raise **objections** if needed, then see the **final key**.
8. If you are in the ranking list, attend **certificate verification** with the filled-in **checklist** and your originals, and give your preference for Government or Local Body schools in your district.

## After you apply {#after}

- **Selection** is on **80 marks of the written test plus 20 marks of TET weightage** (for SA, SGT, Language Pandit and Special Education posts). For School Assistant (Physical Education) and PET, all 100 marks are the written test.
- Recruitment is on **merit cum roster**, with local-candidate reservation under the Presidential Order, and 33⅓% horizontal reservation for women.
- The DSC site publishes hall tickets, keys, response sheets, ranking lists and certificate verification lists. This site lists every new DSC and TET document on **TG DSC & TET: what's new**.

## Good to know

- To be ready for the next DSC, pass the **regular TG TET** (or CTET) for your paper: the Special TET for in-service teachers does not count for recruitment.
- Use only tgdsc.aptonline.in and schooledu.telangana.gov.in for DSC.
