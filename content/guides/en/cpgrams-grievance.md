**CPGRAMS** (Centralised Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System) is the government's official complaint portal. It is connected to all **Central Government** ministries and departments and to the **states**. Use it when a government office has not given you a service it should have.

## Before you start {#before}

- **It is free.** The portal says the government does not charge any fee for filing a grievance. (A Common Service Centre may charge for helping you.)
- **Complaints sent by email are not accepted.** Use the portal, the mobile app or post.
- You need to **register** first. Only registered users can lodge a grievance.

## Documents you will need {#documents}

No fixed list. Keep ready:

- **Your mobile number and email address** (for registering and logging in).
- **Your full address with PIN code.**
- **Any reference numbers** for your problem, such as an application, receipt or account number, and the dates when things happened.

### What CPGRAMS does not handle

- RTI matters (use [RTI Online](/en/guides/rti-online) for those)
- Court cases and matters already before a court
- Religious matters
- Personal and family disputes
- Government employees' service matters, unless they have already used their department's own channels

## Steps {#steps}

1. Open [pgportal.gov.in](https://pgportal.gov.in/) and click **REGISTER / LOGIN**.

   ![The CPGRAMS home page with the "REGISTER / LOGIN" button outlined in red](/static/guides/cpgrams-grievance/1-home.webp "Screenshot of pgportal.gov.in, 14 Oct 2026. The red outline is ours.")

2. New user? Click **Click here to sign up**. (Already registered? Log in with your mobile number, email or username, or with **Login with OTP**.)

   ![The CPGRAMS User Login box, with "Click here to sign up" outlined](/static/guides/cpgrams-grievance/2-signin.webp "Screenshot of pgportal.gov.in, 14 Oct 2026. The red outline is ours.")

3. Fill in the **Registration/Sign up Form**: name, gender, address, country, state, district, PIN code, mobile number and email address, and the security code. Click **Submit**.
4. Log in and choose **Lodge Public Grievance**.
5. Fill in the grievance form: say which ministry, department or state government it is about, and describe your problem clearly (what happened, when, and any application or reference numbers). Submit it.
6. You get a **unique registration number**. Save it.

You can also file using the **CPGRAMS mobile app** (on Google Play and the App Store, and inside UMANG). Without internet, you can send your complaint **by post** on plain paper, a postcard or an inland letter; there is no set format. A Common Service Centre can also file it for you.

## After you file {#after}

- **Track it:** click **View Status** on the portal and enter your registration number.
- **Time limit:** the portal's FAQ says a grievance should be resolved within **21 days**. If there is a delay, the office must send you an interim reply giving the reason.
- **Central Government** complaints go to the ministry or department concerned. **State** complaints are sent to that state's government.
- **No reply in time?** Take it up with the **Director of Public Grievances** of that ministry or department. Their details are on the portal under **Nodal PG Officers**.

## Not happy with the answer? Appeal {#appeal}

1. After your grievance is closed, give your **feedback** (rating).
2. If you rate it **Poor**, the option to **file an appeal** appears.
3. File the appeal **within 30 days**. You can track it under **Appeal Status** with the same registration number.

A closed grievance cannot be re-opened. If you need more details, file a **new** grievance that mentions the old registration number.

## Good to know

- Every **Wednesday**, the Director of Public Grievances in each ministry hears grievances in person.
- Contact: Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances, 5th floor, Sardar Patel Bhavan, Sansad Marg, New Delhi 110001.
