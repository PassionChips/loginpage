---
title: Privacy
---

**We do not ask you for personal information.** The site has no accounts, forms or comments, and we never ask for identity numbers, documents or payment details. Please never send them to us.

**What our server sees.** Like any website, our server receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page. These are used only to keep the site running and to limit abusive traffic, for example by temporarily counting requests from an IP address in memory.

**Cookies.** The site itself does not set cookies.

**Advertising.** If we show ads in future, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure ads. We will update this page before that happens and ask for consent where the law requires it.

**Links.** Official portals and other sites we link to have their own privacy practices.

**Contact.** To ask about this policy or to report an error, use the contact link at the bottom of any guide.
